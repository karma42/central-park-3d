Bethesda Terrace & Fountain Milestone 1 · approximate model

Navigation

Orbit mode

  • Drag: rotate around the target
  • Right-drag or Shift+drag: pan
  • Wheel / pinch / + −: zoom
  • W A S D / arrows: move the target

Walk mode

  • W A S D / arrows: walk (Shift to run)
  • Drag: look around; Q / E: turn
  • Stairs, terraces and the arcade are walkable; the pool and lake are not

Anywhere

  • M: toggle mode · R: reset view · 1–6: viewpoints · H: help

Layout and dimensions are approximate and illustrative; see docs/references.md.
