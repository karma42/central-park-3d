Bethesda Terrace

Orbit mode

  • Drag: rotate around the view centre
  • Right-drag or Shift+drag, two-finger drag: pan
  • Wheel, pinch, +/−: zoom
  • W A S D / arrows: pan; Q E: rotate

Walk mode

  • W A S D / arrows: walk (Shift: run)
  • Drag: look around; Q E: turn
  • Touch: on-screen arrows to move
C or Tab: switch mode · R: reset view
